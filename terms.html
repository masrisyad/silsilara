Silsilara is a free app for recording and exploring family trees. Family tree data and photos are stored in the Google Drive of the family tree owner; a small Silsilara server connects invited family accounts to that family tree and only keeps the data described in the Privacy Policy. 4. Sharing with family

Through Account management, you can give other accounts Admin access (can add, edit, delete, and invite) or View only access. You are responsible for choosing who gets access. Changes and deletions made by Admin accounts apply to everyone who opens that family tree.

If a family tree is shared with you, you can leave it at any time via Profile → Leave this family tree. After leaving, you can't open it again until its owner invites you back.

5. Backups and data deletion

Deleting members and "Reset data" (available only to the family tree's owner) cannot be undone from the app. Deleted photos are moved to the Google Drive Trash and can be restored by their owner while they remain there. We recommend keeping your own backups, for example by downloading the diagram (PNG/PDF) or the silsilah.json file from Google Drive.
