Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · App: Silsilara (Android id.silsilara.app and web)

This policy explains what data Silsilara accesses, why, and where it is stored. In short: Silsilara has no server. Your family tree data is stored only in your Google Drive account (and the family accounts you invite), plus a few settings on your device.

1. Data from your Google account

  • Your Google account name, email address, and profile photo, to show which account is signed in (also in the list of accounts with access) and to record who last changed the family tree.
  • Google Drive access (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), to: create a Silsilara folder containing the silsilah.json file, a photo folder, and the personal settings file pengaturan.json in your Drive; read and update those files; upload family members' photos and move them to the Trash; find family trees that family members share with you; and manage who may open your family tree (the Account management feature).

Silsilara only accesses those Silsilara files and does not read, change, or delete any other files in your Drive.

2. Data you enter

Names, nicknames, gender, dates of birth and death, WhatsApp/phone numbers, photos, and the relationships between family members. When you mark yourself (the "This is you" feature), your Google account email is recorded on that member. All of this data is stored in the silsilah.json file and photo folder in Google Drive, and can be seen by the accounts you give access to.

The family name you set in Profile is your personal version: it is stored in the pengaturan.json file in your own Google Drive and is not shared with other accounts.

3. Data stored on your device

Settings such as light/dark theme, language, the last opened family tree and details, skipped getting-started steps, the "This is you" mark for View only access, and a temporary sign-in token. If birthday reminders are turned on, a list of names and dates of birth of living members is stored on the device so notifications can appear without opening the app. This data is deleted when you sign out or turn off the reminders.

4. Sharing data

Silsilara does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and uses no ads or analytics. Data is only shared with the Google accounts you invite yourself through Account management. The app communicates directly with Google services (Google Drive, Google Sign-In) and loads fonts from Google Fonts. The WhatsApp button on a member's card opens wa.me only when you tap it.

Silsilara's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Deleting data and revoking access

  • Delete a single member, or all members at once via Profile → Reset data.
  • Delete the Silsilara folder from your Google Drive to remove all data and photos.
  • Revoke Silsilara's permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete data on your device by signing out or uninstalling the app.

6. Children

A family tree may contain data about family members who are children. That data is entered and managed by adult family members who have access to the family tree, and is subject to the same policy.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the latest version and its effective date will be updated on this page.
